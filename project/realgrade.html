Real Grade is the most detailed Gundam in the 1/144 scale. Real grade brings most of the features and details found in the larger Master Grade. This added detail at 1/144 does mean that there are some very small parts and so Real Grades can take quite a while to build. If you like the 1/144 size but want more detail in your models then Real Grade is going to scratch that itch.
