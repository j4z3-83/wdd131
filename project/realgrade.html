High grade Gundam are an excellent starting point for your Gunpla journey. They are scaled at 1/144 making them on average 12 to 13 cm tall. High grade have fewer color separated parts preferring stickers for differentiation. They are the least expensive of the Gundam grade, and by far has the largest range allowing you to collect High Grade for years before running out of new kits to experience.

Scale: 1/144
Average Price: $20 - $35 CAD
