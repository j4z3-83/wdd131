Master Grade is larger and more detailed than High Grade Gundam. Master Grade kits are 1/100 scale kits and they include many features not included in High Grade kits, these features include: colour separation for details rather than stickers, a complete inner frame that the armor is then attached to. the Master Grade line is not as large as the High Grade line and some of the kits can be very expensive. If you find High Grade too small and simplistic than the larger scale and improved details of Master Grade will appeal to you.

, Jared Aldridge, Canada
