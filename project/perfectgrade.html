PerFect Grade is the pinnacle of Gundam model kits, at a 1/60 scale these giant kits make a great center piece in anyones collection. Perfect Grade has the most details of any Gundam grade, with features that are not found in the other grades such as metal components, working pistons, and on new Perfect grades optional LED's to light up your giant model. all these great features comes at a price with cheap Perfect Grades starting at $200 CAD and the new Perfect Grade RX-93 costing around $800 CAD. most poeple do not have the space or money to collect Perfect Grade Gundam often finding one or two of their favorite Gundam mobile suits and getting the perfect Grade of that Gundam.
