Super Deformed is more like a sub-genre than a grade of Gundam, it has many lines under the Super Deformed classification, like EX, Legends, the new Master Grade Super Deformed MGSD, and many more. The defining feature of super Deformed is the Chibi styling, with short stubby bodies and oversized heads. They can be the least expensive of all the Gundam grades with SDEX starting at $10 CAD. If you are wanting a quick simple build and cute aesthetics then Super Deformed Gundam will tick all the right boxes.
